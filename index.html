Biography

I am a Research Fellow at NTU's College of Computing and Data Science. My research focuses on AI for database systems and database systems for AI, especially query optimisation, semantic query processing, and heterogeneous and multimodal data systems. I received my PhD in Computer Science from NTU in March 2026. Before pursuing my PhD at NTU, I completed both my bachelor's and master's degrees in Computer Science at Wuhan University.

Research projects

Semantic Query Processing

Execution and optimisation for queries that combine relational operators with model-backed semantic operators, balancing latency, resource use, and result quality.

Heterogeneous and Multimodal Data Systems

Storage, indexing, and real-time query processing across vector, spatial, text, image, and relational data, represented by the open-source ARCADE system.

AI for Database Optimisation

Learning-enhanced optimisation for concurrent and parametric workloads, developed in PostgreSQL and extended to industrial-scale evaluation and integration in OceanBase.

Algorithms for Data-Intensive Applications

Algorithms for graph analytics and urban data applications, including random-walk domination, public-transport scheduling, and trajectory-based advertising.
